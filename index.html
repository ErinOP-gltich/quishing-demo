You scanned a QR code off a stranger's slide and opened an unknown link — without once checking where it pointed. That's all a QR phishing attack needs.

⚠

If this had been real

!

A fake login page could have harvested your email & password.

!

A silent download could have dropped malware on your phone.

!

A fake UPI / payment screen could have taken your money.

🛡

So, next time

✓

Pause before you scan — especially QR codes on posters, tables, and emails.

✓

Preview the URL your camera shows, and read the real domain before you tap.

✓

Never enter a password or pay from a page you reached through a QR.

✓

Treat a QR like a link in an email — it hides where it goes.
